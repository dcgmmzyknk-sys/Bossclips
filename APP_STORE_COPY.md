## Description
Bossclips is a fast, vertical video experience made for discovering entertaining short clips.

Choose your vibe, swipe through a personalized-style feed, like and save favorites, and build your Boss Coin balance as you explore new clips.

Features:
- Full-screen vertical video feed
- Boss Mode, Motivation, Funny and Chill categories
- Likes, saves and sharing
- Watch history and profile stats
- Virtual Boss Coins and progression

Boss Coins are virtual loyalty points only. They have no cash value and cannot be withdrawn, transferred, exchanged for money, or used as cryptocurrency.
