## Information we may collect
We may collect account information, app activity such as viewed/liked/saved clips, device and diagnostic information, and support communications. If creator uploads are enabled, we may process uploaded media and moderation reports.

## How we use information
We use information to operate the app, personalize the feed, maintain security, prevent abuse, provide virtual Boss Coin rewards, troubleshoot problems, and improve the service.

## Boss Coins
Boss Coins are virtual loyalty points with no cash value. They cannot be withdrawn, transferred, sold, exchanged for money, or used as cryptocurrency.

## Sharing

## Retention and deletion
